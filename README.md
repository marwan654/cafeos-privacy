# Privacy Policy for CafeOS Apps

Last updated: 8 / 10 /2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **CafeOS** , handles information through the CafeOS Owner and CafeOS Cashier applications and their supporting services.

**Privacy contact:** cafeos.team@gmail.com

## 1. Scope and Responsibilities

CafeOS provides tools for managing establishments, branches, employees, products, inventory, sales, invoices, and customer loyalty programs.

This policy applies to establishment owners, authorized employees, and individuals whose information is processed through these services.

Establishments decide what employee and customer information they enter into CafeOS and how they use it for their business. We process that information to provide the service on their behalf. We also handle information for our own service administration, security, and support.

If you are a customer or employee of an establishment using CafeOS, you may contact that establishment about its handling of your information.

## 2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on the application, your role, and the features your establishment uses.

### Account and establishment information

This includes owner and employee names, login identifiers, authentication information, roles, permissions, branch assignments, establishment contact information, and business details.

Business details may include the establishment’s legal name, address, commercial registration information, and tax registration number.

### Device and pairing information

We process device identifiers and pairing records needed to connect authorized devices to an establishment and manage access. Records may include device names, device types, and pairing or activity timestamps.

### Sales and operational information

CafeOS processes products, prices, inventory movements, orders, transaction amounts, payment methods, receipts, invoices, and records identifying the employee or branch responsible for an operation.

Order information may include customer names and notes entered by an authorized user. Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information in free-text fields.

We process product images users choose to upload and information voluntarily provided when contacting support, such as messages and screenshots.

## 4. Camera, Photos, and Local Network Access

Certain features require access to device capabilities:

- **Camera:** Used to scan activation QR codes when you choose this option.
- **Photos or files:** Used to read an activation QR image or select a product image. Product images selected for upload are sent to the service so they can appear on authorized devices.
- **Local network:** Used by supported printing features to communicate with configured receipt printers.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Restricting a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

## 5. Payments and Electronic Invoicing

CafeOS records transaction amounts, payment methods, and payment status.

Recording a card payment in the cashier app is separate from processing the card payment through an external payment terminal. Users should not enter card numbers, security codes, or banking passwords into order notes or support messages.

When electronic invoicing is enabled, the service processes the establishment and transaction information required to generate and submit invoices to the relevant tax authority, including Saudi Arabia’s Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority where applicable.
